Why bartenders love rum

Flexible, friendly with fruit and citrus, and almost endless in style.

Ask bartenders what they like about rum and the answers are surprisingly similar. It is a spirit with room to play.

It works with almost everything

Rum pairs naturally with lime, lemon, pineapple, coconut, mint, ginger and coffee. It is as comfortable in a fresh, sour drink as in a long, fizzy one. That makes it easy to build both simple serves and complicated ones.

One category, many personalities

A bar can keep a light rum, an aged one and a spiced one and cover very different drinks. Few categories stretch from a clean Daiquiri to a rich Old Fashioned.

A natural sweetness

Because rum comes from sugarcane, many styles carry a rounded sweetness. That helps with balance: less sugar syrup is needed, and drinks feel generous without being heavy.

Fun to serve

Rum is tied to holidays, beaches and good company, and bartenders know guests enjoy that mood. Tropical garnishes and tall glasses do half the work.

Easy for guests to enjoy

A rum and soda with citrus is approachable for people who do not drink spirits often, which makes it a reliable choice at home as well as in a bar.
