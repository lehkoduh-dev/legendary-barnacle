Best rum for Rum and Ginger Beer

A spiced, slightly sharp long drink. What rum suits ginger beer, and a Beach Heaven twist with Spiced Velvet.

Looking for the best rum for rum and ginger beer? A rum with some warmth and spice, because ginger beer is sharp and a little sweet. Dark or spiced rums are the usual choice.

The classic serve

Rum and ginger beer is a simple long drink: rum, ginger beer and a squeeze of lime over ice in a tall glass.

What makes a rum good with ginger beer

Ginger is bold, so pick a rum with its own character, ideally with spice or citrus. Light, delicate rums get lost.

The Beach Heaven twist: Velvet & Ginger

Spiced Velvet carries orange and lime notes, which lift the ginger and finish the drink with a citrus edge.

  • 50 ml Beach Heaven Spiced Velvet
  • 10 ml fresh lime juice
  • 120 ml ginger beer
  • Plenty of ice

Fill a highball glass with ice, add Spiced Velvet and lime juice, top with ginger beer and stir once, slowly. Garnish with a lime wedge.

Tips

Use a good ginger beer with real ginger bite, serve it very cold and keep the stirring to a minimum.
