Rum, step by step

How rum is made.

Four steps from sugarcane to bottle.

Sugarcane or molasses

Rum starts with sugarcane. Most rum is made from molasses, the dark syrup left when sugar is made. Some is made from fresh cane juice.

Fermentation

Yeast turns the sugars into alcohol. A shorter fermentation gives a lighter rum, a longer one a fuller, more flavourful rum.

Distillation

The liquid is heated and the alcohol vapour is collected and cooled. Column stills give a lighter spirit, pot stills a richer one.

Ageing and blending

Rum can rest in barrels, or be bottled young. Blenders then combine rums to a consistent style, and flavours can be added for a spirit drink.
