FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short answers about Beach Heaven.

What is Beach Heaven?

Beach Heaven is a rum-based tropical spirit drink in three blends: Pink Dream, Lychee Bliss and Spiced Velvet.

How strong is it?

All three blends are 37.5% vol. and come in a 70 cl bottle.

What do the blends taste like?

Pink Dream has notes of hibiscus, grapefruit and blueberry. Lychee Bliss has lychee, lemon, lime, cucumber and ginger. Spiced Velvet has orange and lime.

Who makes Beach Heaven?

Beach Heaven is a product of Kroonbrau BV, Amsterdam.

How should I serve it?

Pink Dream goes over ice with a squeeze of citrus, or long with soda or tonic. Lychee Bliss is lovely chilled over ice, with soda and a lime wedge. Spiced Velvet works on the rocks, or with ginger beer and lime. See all the cocktails.

Which rum is best for a Daiquiri, a Mojito or a Piña Colada?

The classics call for a light white rum, so we say so in our journal and give a Beach Heaven twist for each: Pink Dream for a Daiquiri, Lychee Bliss for a Mojito and Spiced Velvet for a Piña Colada.
