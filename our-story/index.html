Our story

Made for mixing.

Good taste first, and made with cocktails in mind.

Beach Heaven is a rum-based tropical spirit drink made with good taste first. It is smooth enough to sip, and it was created with cocktails in mind.

A twist on the classics

Pink Dream, Lychee Bliss and Spiced Velvet bring their own flavour to well-known cocktails, so a Daiquiri, a Mojito or a Piña Colada becomes something new.

Easy with any mixer

Soda, tonic, ginger beer, lemonade, cola: pour, add ice, done. No bar skills or long ingredient lists.

Great drinks at home, in minutes

A good cocktail should not take an evening.
