Cocktail · Made with Spiced Velvet

Velvet & Cola

A familiar long drink, made warmer.

  • Serves 1
  • Easy
  • Sweet
  • 1 min
  • Highball glass
  • Garnish: Lime wedge
About Spiced VelvetAll cocktails

Ingredients

What you need.

  • 50 ml Beach Heaven Spiced Velvet
  • 100 ml Chilled cola
  • 1 Lime wedge
  • Ice

Method

How to make it.

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add Spiced Velvet.
  3. Top with cola and squeeze in the lime wedge.
