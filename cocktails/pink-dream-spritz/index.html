Pink Dream Spritz

Pink, bubbly and made for long afternoons.

  • Serves 1
  • Easy
  • Bubbly
  • 2 min
  • Wine glass
  • Garnish: Orange slice
About Pink DreamAll cocktails

Ingredients

What you need.

  • 40 ml Beach Heaven Pink Dream
  • 80 ml Chilled sparkling wine
  • 40 ml Soda water
  • 1 Orange slice
  • Ice

Method

How to make it.

  1. Fill a wine glass with ice.
  2. Add Pink Dream, then the sparkling wine.
  3. Top with soda water and stir once.
  4. Add the orange slice.
