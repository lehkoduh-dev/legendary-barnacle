Cocktail · Made with Spiced Velvet

Velvet & Ginger

Warm amber and sharp ginger beer. Built in the glass in under a minute.

  • Serves 1
  • Easy
  • Spicy
  • 2 min
  • Highball glass
  • Garnish: Lime wedge
About Spiced VelvetAll cocktails

Ingredients

What you need.

  • 50 ml Beach Heaven Spiced Velvet
  • 120 ml Ginger beer
  • 10 ml Fresh lime juice
  • Ice

Method

How to make it.

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add Spiced Velvet and lime juice.
  3. Top with ginger beer and give it one slow stir.
