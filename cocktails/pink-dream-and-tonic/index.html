Pink Dream & Tonic

Long, light and pink. The easiest way into Pink Dream.

  • Serves 1
  • Easy
  • Refreshing
  • 1 min
  • Highball glass
  • Garnish: Grapefruit wedge
About Pink DreamAll cocktails

Ingredients

What you need.

  • 50 ml Beach Heaven Pink Dream
  • 150 ml Chilled tonic water
  • 1 Grapefruit wedge
  • Plenty of ice

Method

How to make it.

  1. Fill a highball glass to the top with ice.
  2. Pour in Pink Dream.
  3. Top with chilled tonic water and stir once, gently.
  4. Squeeze the grapefruit wedge over the drink and drop it in.
