Cocktail · Made with Spiced Velvet

Spiced Velvet Old Fashioned

A slow, stirred sipper with orange oils.

  • Serves 1
  • Intermediate
  • Bold
  • 3 min
  • Rocks glass
  • Garnish: Orange peel
About Spiced VelvetAll cocktails

Ingredients

What you need.

  • 50 ml Beach Heaven Spiced Velvet
  • 5 ml Simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Orange bitters
  • 1 Large ice cube
  • Ice for stirring

Method

How to make it.

  1. Add Spiced Velvet, syrup and bitters to a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir for 20 to 30 seconds, until the glass is very cold.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a rocks glass.
  4. Squeeze a strip of orange peel over the drink, then drop it in.
