Cocktail · Made with Lychee Bliss

Lychee Bliss Mojito

Mint, lime and ripe lychee over crushed ice.

  • Serves 1
  • Intermediate
  • Refreshing
  • 4 min
  • Highball glass
  • Garnish: Mint sprig
About Lychee BlissAll cocktails

Ingredients

What you need.

  • 50 ml Beach Heaven Lychee Bliss
  • 25 ml Fresh lime juice
  • 10 ml Simple syrup
  • 8 Fresh mint leaves
  • Soda water to top
  • Crushed ice

Method

How to make it.

  1. Add mint leaves, lime juice and simple syrup to the glass and press the mint gently, without tearing it.
  2. Pour in Lychee Bliss.
  3. Fill the glass with crushed ice and stir from the bottom up.
  4. Top with soda water and add a little more ice on top.

Enjoy responsibly.
